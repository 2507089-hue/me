LIVE IN PRODUCTION

LUX EMPIRE

From an idea to a live product. Started 5 May 2026 as a personal project; live on 7 October 2026.

Visit live website: luxempire.co.ke
https://luxempire.co.ke/
Home page, captured from the live site. Select to enlarge.Open luxempire.co.ke

The problem

Finding a home and arranging transport usually means separate searches, calls, and payments. LUX EMPIRE puts property discovery, bookings, moving requests, communication, and payments in one place.

Who it serves

Tenants find and book homes. Landlords list and manage properties. Drivers accept moving requests. Administrators oversee users, listings, and bookings.

Platform capabilities

Property

Listings, discovery, bookings, and landlord management.

Moving

Truck requests, driver acceptance, trip-status workflows, and map features.

Payments

M-PESA-related workflows, booking and payment-status handling.

Communication

In-platform messaging and notifications.

Administration

Tools for users, listings, bookings, and operational issues.

Safeguards

Authentication, rate limiting, validation, and application logging.

Engineering

InterfaceJavaScript and CSS, light and dark themes
ApplicationPHP classes, controllers, services, APIs
DataMariaDB through PDO, schema files
BackgroundWorker scripts: notifications, refunds, media
ProductionLinux server deployment

Security and reliability are part of the engineering work here, not a guarantee. The platform keeps being tested and improved.
